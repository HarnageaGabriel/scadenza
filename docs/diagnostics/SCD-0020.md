# SCD-0020: Scan did not check everything it was asked to check

- Slug: `incomplete-scan`
- Severity: `error`

## Explanation

Either --strict found a partial or failed source, a failed probe, or an unchecked name, or the coverage ratio fell below --min-coverage. The results are still complete for what was checked; the scan is simply not a clean one.

## Remediation

Review the coverage summary and unresolved names, then raise --max-hosts, fix the failing source or probe, or lower --min-coverage once the gap is understood.
