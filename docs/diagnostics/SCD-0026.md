# SCD-0026: Renewal health is degraded

- Slug: `renewal-degraded`
- Severity: `warning`

## Explanation

A cert-manager Certificate reports Ready=False, status.renewalTime passed more than one hour before the evaluation instant, or status.notAfter has passed. The certificate is not being renewed as expected.

## Remediation

Inspect the Certificate conditions and issuer, and confirm the certificate is renewed before status.notAfter.
